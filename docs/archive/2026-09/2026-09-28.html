Russia intensified drone and long-range strikes across Ukraine on September 27, killing at least eight civilians including children and destroying critical data centers and logistics infrastructure—the latest escalation in the fifth-year conflict, though Ukrainian counterattacks are reportedly pushing both sides toward negotiations.
